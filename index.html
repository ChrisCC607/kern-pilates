I've never done Pilates. Will I keep up?

Everyone in the room is starting from the same place. It's mat-based — no intimidating machines — and every movement scales to you. You won't be behind; you'll be working.

No. It's one hour a week that makes everything else work better — more range, a stronger base, fewer tweaks. Keep lifting and running exactly as you do.

Men only, capped at 8. No mirrors full of strangers, no being the odd one out — just guys who train and want the same thing you do.
