I've never done Pilates. Will I keep up?

Everyone in the room is starting from the same place. It's mat-based — no intimidating machines — and every movement scales to you. You won't be behind; you'll be working.

Does this replace my gym training?

No — it's built to slot into your training week. It makes everything else work better: shoulder mobility for a bigger overhead press, t-spine for your bench, open hips for a deeper squat. Come as often as you train, and keep your push-pull-legs and runs exactly as they are.

Who's actually in the room?

People who train. KERN is built and programmed for men — and women are welcome too. Everyone starts from the same place, so you're never the odd one out, just people who want the same thing you do.

What do I bring, and what's the cost?

Just yourself, water, and clothes you can move in. Mats are provided. Your first class is $25, paid up front to hold your spot.
